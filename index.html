FAQ — Scent Company

Frequently Asked Questions

Key information about our service model, installation process, maintenance, fragrance changes and professional scent diffusion systems.

Is there a minimum contract period?

There is no minimum contract period. The service can be canceled at any time, at no aditional cost.

Who is responsible for the installation?

Our team takes care of the full installation process. We analyze the areas to be scented, recommend the most efficient diffusion system, and install the equipment in a non-invasive way, always respecting the design and structure of the space.

How does maintenance work?

Our service is all-inclusive. Maintenance, perfume refills, system adjustments, and ongoing support are handled by Scent Company, so your team does not need to manage technical issues or operational details.

Can I change the fragrance?

Yes. Fragrance changes are part of the service experience. We can help you select a new scent according to your brand identity, seasonality, customer experience goals, or the atmosphere you want to create.

Does the system work with my existing HVAC structure?

Yes, when the space allows it. Our professional diffusion systems can be connected to existing air conditioning ducts to distribute the fragrance evenly throughout the environment. We also offer mobile diffusion systems for spaces where HVAC integration is not the best option.

What makes Scent Company different from regular air fresheners?

Scent Company creates professional olfactory branding solutions, not generic air freshening. Our fragrances are developed in Italy using high-quality raw materials, and our systems are designed to deliver a consistent, elegant, and controlled scent experience.

What is included in the monthly service?

The service includes the scent diffusion system, fragrance supply, refills, maintenance, system support, and adjustments. It is designed as a full-service solution with no operational burden for the client.
