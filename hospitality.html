FAQ — Hospitality Scenting

Frequently Asked Questions

Key information about professional scenting, installation, maintenance, fragrance adjustments and diffusion for hospitality environments.

Does scent work in large hotel lobbies?

Yes. Professional diffusion systems can be calibrated according to lobby size, airflow, traffic and operating hours to maintain a balanced fragrance experience.

Can different areas have different scent levels?

Yes. Intensity and frequency can be adjusted by area, allowing the lobby, spa, corridors and social spaces to maintain different levels of presence.

Will the scent interfere with restaurants or food service?

No. Hospitality scenting should be carefully calibrated so it enhances atmosphere without overpowering food, beverage or social interaction.

Is installation disruptive to hotel operations?

The systems are designed for professional, non-invasive integration and can be planned around operational routines.

Is maintenance included?

Yes. The service is all-inclusive, with refills, maintenance, system support and adjustments managed by Scent Company.

Can we change the fragrance later?

Yes. If the selected fragrance is not the right fit, or if you want to create a different atmosphere for a season, event or brand refresh, we can recommend alternative scent directions.
