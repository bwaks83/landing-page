No. Scent Company does not require a long-term contract commitment for this service model. Our approach is designed to give your business flexibility from day one.

Are there cancellation fees?

No. There are no cancellation fees. Premium scenting should give your business confidence, not make you feel locked into a provider.

What makes your fragrance quality different?

Our fragrances are crafted with an Italian luxury sensibility, designed to deliver a more refined fragrance experience aligned with premium environments.

Can you replace an existing scent provider?

Yes. We can review your current setup, recommend the right diffusion system and fragrance direction, and help you transition to a more premium, flexible service model.

What is included in the Free Scent Upgrade Audit?

The audit includes a short consultation, review of your space or current scenting model, fragrance direction recommendations and the next technical step for installation or replacement.
