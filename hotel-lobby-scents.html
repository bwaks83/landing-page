FAQ — Hotel Lobby Scents

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about selecting, installing and managing a professional signature fragrance for your hotel lobby.

What scents work best in hotel lobbies?

The best profile depends on your brand and atmosphere. Citrus and clean fragrances feel bright and fresh; woody and amber profiles feel warm and luxurious; floral scents can feel elegant; and spa-inspired profiles support calm and wellbeing.

How do we choose a signature scent for our hotel?

We consider your architecture, location, guest profile, brand personality and desired emotional impression, then recommend a fragrance direction and intensity for evaluation.

Can the lobby scenting system connect to our existing HVAC?

Yes, when the property layout and air-handling system are suitable. HVAC connection can provide even lobby coverage. Where it is not the best fit, we can recommend a discreet standalone system.

How much does professional hotel lobby scenting cost?

All-inclusive plans start at $180 per month per diffuser. Final pricing depends on lobby size, airflow, coverage requirements, fragrance usage and the recommended installation approach.

Are fragrance, refills and maintenance included?

Yes. The managed service includes fragrance, scheduled refills, equipment maintenance, system support and intensity adjustments.
